# POKéDEX

A retro-inspired, interactive Pokémon Pokédex built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Explore Pokémon from different regions through a custom Pokédex interface inspired by the classic handheld games.

## Features

* ** Multiple Regions ** — Browse Pokémon by region, with dedicated regional themes.
* ** National Pokédex ** — Access the National Pokédex to explore Pokémon beyond individual regions.
* ** Additional Pokédexes ** — Explore other Pokédex collections, including Mega and Gigantamax entries.
* ** Search Functionality ** — Search for Pokémon by name or Pokédex number.
* ** Retro UI Design ** — A handheld Pokédex interface featuring a screen, directional pad, keypad, and interactive buttons.
* ** Shiny Pokémon ** — View shiny Pokémon sprites.
* ** Pokémon Cries ** — Listen to Pokémon cries.
* ** Random Pokémon ** — Discover a random Pokémon.
* ** Pokémon Information ** — View details such as species, height, weight, types, and descriptions.
* ** Regional Themes ** — Custom CSS themes for different Pokémon regions.

## Technologies Used

* ** HTML5 ** — Page structure and Pokédex interface.
* ** CSS3 ** — Styling, layout, and regional themes.
* ** JavaScript ** — Application logic, navigation, search, and interactive features.
* ** [PokéAPI](https://pokeapi.co/) ** — Pokémon data source.

## How to Use

1. Open the Pokédex and choose a region.
2. Select a Pokédex collection.
3. Search for a Pokémon using its name or number.
4. Use the directional controls to navigate between entries.
5. View Pokémon information, including its sprite, type, height, weight, and description.
6. Try the ** RND ** button to discover a random Pokémon.
7. Use ** SHINY ** to explore shiny sprites and ** CRY ** to listen to Pokémon cries.
8. Use ** CLR ** to clear the search and ** GO ** to submit your selection.

## Data Source

Pokémon information is sourced from [PokéAPI](https://pokeapi.co/), a free and open RESTful API providing Pokémon-related data.
